## Header Requirements

## Interactive header mega-menu requirements

The enhanced header dropdown is a deliberate showpiece and must remain substantially more interactive than a basic list of navigation links.

### Desktop Services dropdown

- Preserve the full-width Services mega-menu and its exact 50/50 two-column composition.
- The left column is the service selector. Every service needs a clear hover and keyboard-focus highlight, an identifying number, a title, and a short system/category label.
- The right column is an interactive presentation stage. Hovering or focusing a service must update its title, description, metric, detailed capability list, destination URL, and distinct visual treatment.
- Every item in `.service-stage__capabilities` is a real link. Its URL fragment must match a visible capability-card ID in the Services directory, and hover/focus changes the item to royal/navy blue with cream-white lettering.
- left and right internal spacing must remain equal at desktop breakpoints.
- Keep the orbit, data, transition, and pointer-depth effects polished and purposeful. New designs should meet or exceed this level of interaction rather than reducing the dropdown to a conventional card grid.
- Services must contain useful detail. Each primary service should normally expose between three up to 12 specific capabilities.
- Desktop pointer interaction, keyboard focus, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, and reliable link destinations are all required.
- Hover continues to provide temporary previews. Clicking a main navigation trigger pins that dropdown open until the user clicks the same trigger, another main trigger, or anywhere outside the mega-menu. Do not remove either interaction mode.

### Mobile Services dropdown

- The hamburger menu must remain a usable scrollable navigation surface.
- After the user expands Services, show every service and its detailed capabilities together. Do not make mobile users hover, select one active service, or depend on the desktop right-hand stage to discover the other services.
- Mobile service entries are an all-expanded catalog: each entry shows its title, description, category, and complete capability list, with a clear path to its service destination.
- Mobile service cards use the warm cream background with royal/navy-blue typography. Orange is reserved for the `.service-option__mobile-cta`; numbers, labels, arrows, body copy, and capability text remain in the blue family.
- The desktop-only `is-active` interaction may still exist in the markup and JavaScript, but it must not hide content or create a single-selected-service presentation on mobile.
- Responsive changes must preserve the interactive desktop experience; do not solve mobile behavior by simplifying or removing the desktop mega-menu.

### Blog dropdown

- Treat the Blog dropdown as a finished editorial showcase, not a plain archive list.
- Query live WordPress posts and support sparse or full content sets gracefully. One to four returned posts must produce a balanced grid without empty visual columns.
- Blog cards need polished hover and keyboard-focus feedback, readable contrast, clamped variable-length content, and responsive one- or two-column layouts inside the mobile navigation.

### Work dropdown

- Keep `#nytt99-mega-work` visually and structurally distinct from About and every other dropdown. It is a selected-work showcase, not a generic overview panel.
- Keep the Work panel's full-width outer background consistent with the other mega menus: the established navy surface with cream-white text. The white artifact viewer remains the contrasting right-hand focal point.
- Desktop uses an exact 50/50 split: a five-track project index on the left and an interactive preview lab on the right.
- The five required tracks are Website Showcases, Plugin Projects, AI Agent Setups, AI Business Workflows, and GitHub Projects. Each includes a useful description, supporting project types, and a reliable destination.
- Hover and keyboard focus on a track must update the preview title, description, code, project types, destination, position, and track-specific visual treatment. Every option also remains a direct link.
- Active, hover, and focus backgrounds in the left project index must remain fully contained inside that column; they must never bleed to the viewport edge.
- Desktop Work project options use a white active, hover, and keyboard-focus surface with navy text and a royal-blue directional arrow against the navy panel background.
- The right-hand `.work-lab` uses five crisp, purpose-built artifact scenes rather than reshaping one generic preview: website canvas, plugin architecture, governed agent network, business workflow, and repository/code view.
- Mobile keeps all five tracks readable without hover or JavaScript. The nonessential desktop preview lab may be hidden, but descriptions and project types must remain visible.
- Work-specific changes must remain scoped to `.mega-panel--work`, `.work-mega`, `.work-option`, and `.work-lab`; do not restyle or restructure Services, About, or Blog to implement Work changes.

### Header CTA

- The primary header action uses the dedicated `.header-btn-cta` class, the label “Contact Us,” a royal-blue background, and warm off-white text. Keep its styles in `src/scss/layout/header.scss` rather than changing the shared `.button` component.

The Services menu content is defined in `inc/navigation.php`, its interaction is handled in `src/js/components/site-navigation.js`, and its presentation is maintained in `src/scss/layout/header.scss`. Keep those three layers synchronized whenever menu content or behavior changes.
